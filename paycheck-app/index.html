Pay settings

Backup

Your shifts are saved on this iPhone only. Save a backup now and then (it goes to the Files app) so you never lose them.

Breaks: any shift longer than 5 hours automatically has a 30-minute unpaid break taken out. Check "No unpaid break this shift" when you log a shift to skip it.

Overtime: hours over 40 in a single week (each half of a pay period) are paid at 1.5× your rate, per NJ law.

Pay periods: two weeks long. Set any date a period starts on and how many days after it ends you get paid. Set from your Proliant stub: periods run Monday to Sunday and you're paid the Friday after (5 days later).

Take-home estimate: wages and tips minus the withholding %. The 10% default covers Social Security and Medicare (7.65%), NJ income tax, and NJ payroll deductions. With part-time earnings, federal income tax withholding is usually close to zero. Your real stub will differ a bit. Credit card tips come through your paycheck, so the same % is taken out of them. Cash tips go straight in your pocket with nothing withheld. "With cash tips" is your paycheck plus your cash. Cash tips are still taxable income when you file.
